Your Program
🎯 Built For You
Active basketball player, two full-court leagues. Solid cardio base, strong legs. Focus: upper body pulling strength, posterior chain (lower back, glutes, hamstrings), core stability. 5 days active + 2 rest. Tap the small ▶ on any exercise to watch a form video.
Back Health Keys
🔑
Hip Flexor Stretch — Every Day
Basketball tightens hip flexors constantly. Tight hip flexors pull your lower back forward — one of the top causes of back pain in active people.
45–60 sec each side, morning and night
🔑
Posterior Chain = Back Protection
Basketball heavily trains your quads but under-trains glutes and hamstrings. A strong posterior chain takes load off your spine and protects your back long-term.
Squeeze glutes hard at the top of every bridge and deadlift
🔑
Pain vs Good Discomfort
Muscle burn and fatigue are expected and fine. Sharp, shooting, or joint pain means stop immediately. Never push through back pain — swap for a stretch day instead.
When in doubt, rest it out
🏈
This Helps Your Game
Pull-up strength, core stability, and posterior chain work directly improve your vertical, your defensive positioning, and your on-court endurance.
Think of this as basketball-specific cross-training
